# Content Review

Perform a ruthless but constructive review of the provided content against the
`brutal-critic` rubric and the writer skill's standards.

## Review Process

1. **Read Completely**: Understand full context before judging
2. **Rubric Check**: Apply the four scoring dimensions and their anchors
3. **Standards Check**: Verify claims, sourcing, and channel defaults, citing the rule
4. **Strength Assessment**: Identify what genuinely works
5. **Weakness Identification**: Quote the line and name the issue
6. **Improvement Suggestions**: Fix plus expected outcome for each finding
7. **Scoring**: Rate Content Quality, Structure, Engagement, Overall (/10 each)

## Scoring Guidelines

- 9-10: Exceptional — meets the rubric at publish quality
- 7-8: Good — minor improvements needed
- 5-6: Average — significant work required
- 3-4: Poor — major structural issues
- 1-2: Unacceptable — fundamental problems
